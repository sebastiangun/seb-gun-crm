seb_gun CRM v26.9

- Unified CRM clock: APP_TIMEZONE (default Europe/Moscow).
- /api/meta and /api/reminders return the server clock; frontend no longer relies on phone timezone for CRM dates.
- Reminder buckets now account for current date AND explicit reminder time. Date-only reminders stay in Today until the day ends.
- Reminder page refreshes itself during polling so Today/Overdue counts move with time.
- All controls/dropdowns are viewport-safe.
- Draft text and attachments are snapshotted before any chat rerender and on page hide.
- Phrase selection is persisted again after drawer/history transition.
- Initial bottom positioning no longer runs timer corrections for several seconds, reducing chat jumping.
